HOMEHUB APPLIANCES
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Where does HomeHub Appliances operate?

A: HomeHub currently provides standard delivery and selected
installation services within Karachi. Delivery outside Karachi may
be available depending on the product and destination.

Q: How can I request warranty service?

A: Customers can contact HomeHub customer support with their proof
of purchase and relevant product information.

Q: How quickly are orders delivered within Karachi?

A: Standard delivery normally takes 2–4 business days after order
confirmation, subject to stock availability and operational
conditions.

Q: Is installation always free?

A: No. Complimentary installation applies only to eligible products
and standard installation requirements. Additional work may incur
charges.

Q: Can I return an appliance after using it?

A: Used or installed products may not qualify for a standard return.
Return eligibility depends on the applicable return policy and
condition of the product.

Q: Does every product have the same warranty?

A: No. Warranty periods vary by product and may also differ between
the complete appliance and individual components.

Q: What should I do if my appliance arrives visibly damaged?

A: Customers should report visible delivery damage promptly,
preferably within 24 hours, and retain the original packaging and
delivery documentation.
