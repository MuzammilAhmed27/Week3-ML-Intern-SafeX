REFRIGERATOR TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Problem: Refrigerator is not cooling properly

Possible checks:
1. Confirm that the refrigerator is receiving electrical power.
2. Check whether the temperature setting is appropriate.
3. Make sure the refrigerator doors are closing completely.
4. Avoid blocking internal air vents with food.
5. Check whether the condenser area requires cleaning according to
   the manufacturer's instructions.

If the refrigerator continues to operate incorrectly after basic
checks, customers should contact authorized service support.

Problem: Refrigerator makes unusual sounds

Some operating sounds are normal, including compressor operation,
fan noise, and occasional clicking associated with automatic
defrosting.

Persistent unusual sounds, especially when accompanied by cooling
problems, should be inspected by qualified service personnel.

Safety:
Customers should not attempt to repair electrical or sealed
refrigeration components themselves.
