AIR CONDITIONER TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Problem: Air conditioner is not cooling effectively

Check:
1. Whether the selected temperature is appropriate.
2. Whether doors and windows are closed when cooling is required.
3. Whether the indoor air filter requires cleaning.
4. Whether the outdoor unit has unobstructed airflow.
5. Whether the unit is receiving stable electrical power.

If cooling performance remains poor, professional inspection may
be required.

Problem: Indoor unit is leaking water

Switch off the unit and contact qualified service personnel if water
leakage continues.

Customers should not open refrigerant circuits or attempt refrigerant
charging themselves.

Problem: Remote control does not work

Check the batteries and ensure there are no objects blocking the
remote-control signal path.
