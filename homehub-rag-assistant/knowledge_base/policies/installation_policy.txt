HOMEHUB APPLIANCES
INSTALLATION POLICY

Standard Installation

Eligible refrigerators and washing machines purchased from HomeHub
may include complimentary standard installation within Karachi.

Eligible air conditioner purchases include standard installation
subject to the product and order conditions.

Standard installation covers normal installation requirements.

Additional work may result in additional charges.

Examples of potentially chargeable work include extended copper
piping, major electrical modifications, structural drilling beyond
standard requirements, specialized mounting, or other non-standard
installation requirements.

Microwave ovens generally do not require professional installation
for normal operation.

Customers should confirm installation requirements before purchase
if the installation location has unusual electrical, structural,
or access requirements.
