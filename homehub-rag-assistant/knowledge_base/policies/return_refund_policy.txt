HOMEHUB APPLIANCES
RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery
for eligible products.

Returned products must generally be unused and include the original
packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof of purchase.

Products that have been installed, modified, physically damaged,
or used may not qualify for a standard return.

Customers should report visible delivery damage as soon as possible,
preferably within 24 hours of receiving the product.

Approved returns are inspected before a refund or replacement is
authorized.

Refund processing time depends on the original payment method and
may require several business days after return approval.

Certain clearance, customized, or specially ordered products may
have different return conditions.

For questions regarding eligibility, customers should contact
HomeHub customer support before sending a product back.
