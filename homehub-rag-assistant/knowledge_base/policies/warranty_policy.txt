HOMEHUB APPLIANCES
WARRANTY POLICY

General Warranty

Warranty coverage applies only to products purchased directly from
HomeHub Appliances or through an authorized HomeHub sales channel.

Warranty periods vary by product and may include separate coverage
for the complete appliance and specific components such as compressors
or inverter motors.

Warranty does not normally cover damage caused by misuse, physical
impact, unauthorized modification, incorrect installation, electrical
surges, accidents, or failure to follow manufacturer instructions.

Warranty claims require valid proof of purchase.

Component-specific warranties do not automatically mean that every
other component of the appliance is covered for the same period.

For exact coverage, customers should refer to the product-specific
warranty information and applicable manufacturer terms.

Service requests can be initiated through HomeHub customer support.
