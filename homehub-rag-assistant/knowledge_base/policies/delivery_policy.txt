HOMEHUB APPLIANCES
DELIVERY POLICY

Standard Delivery

Standard delivery within Karachi normally takes 2–4 business days
after order confirmation.

Delivery schedules may be affected by public holidays, severe
weather, traffic conditions, stock availability, or other operational
issues.

Delivery outside Karachi may require additional time and delivery
charges.

Customers should provide an accurate delivery address and contact
number.

Large appliances may require the customer to ensure that building
access, elevators, stairways, and installation areas are accessible.

Delivery personnel may not be able to complete delivery when safe
access to the destination is unavailable.

Customers should inspect the external condition of delivered
appliances and report visible damage promptly.
